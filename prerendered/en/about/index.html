— ABOUT US —

One brand — an entire industry

OenoHub.ge was born of a simple idea: the Georgian wine industry deserves a unified, modern, interconnected ecosystem — from vineyard to the final product.

— OUR STORY —

How the ecosystem took shape

Georgia is the cradle of wine — with an 8,000-year tradition. But tradition alone is not enough: a modern industry demands technology, quality packaging, logistics and education. These gaps were visible in the daily work of thousands of Georgian winemakers.

The OenoHub.ge ecosystem was created precisely to fill these gaps — step by step: first digital tools for viticulture and oenology, then European supplies of packaging and filtration, logistics and consulting, and finally international education.

Today, 11 companies work as a single organism. Each is a leader in its field; together they form a complete chain the Georgian wine industry has never had before.

Mission

To deliver tools, quality and knowledge to every player in the wine and agro industry — from a single trusted source.

Vision

The Caucasus as a modern center of the wine industry — where tradition and technology strengthen one another.

Values

Quality without compromise · Transparent partnership · Sharing knowledge · Sustainable development.

A circle that never begins and never ends

TechnologyProductionPackagingLogisticsEducation

Our ecosystem is not linear — it is circular. Education creates professionals who use our technology; technology grows production; production needs packaging and logistics — and everything returns once more to knowledge.

— THE JOURNEY —

Stages of the ecosystem’s formation

  1. 01

    The beginning — Wine Hub

    It all began with Wine Hub — the distribution of small and medium wineries. Then Winelens.ge and Oeno.ge laid the digital foundation of the wine industry, followed by VineAI.ge and AgroAI.ge — AI in viticulture and agriculture.

  2. 02

    The packaging stage

    Vidrala.ge, AggloTap.ge and PortugalCork.ge: three pillars of European packaging — glass, technical and natural cork.

  3. 03

    Filtration & quality

    Filtrox.ge joined the ecosystem with its standard of wine purity.

  4. 04

    Logistics & expertise

    Primelogistics.ge’s 200 m² warehouse and GS Consulting’s strategic team joined the chain.

  5. 05

    The education stage

    The International Sommelier Guild and Edinburgh Whisky Academy franchises for the Caucasus — closing the ecosystem’s full circle.
